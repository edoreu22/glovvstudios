Who we are

GLOVV Studios is a small indie team and the main developers behind Pokémon Void. It all started with a dream from YouTuber PokedexFillers. Through our partnership, we helped create the free fan experience Pokémon Void, and it's the first of many projects to come.

We're planning more than fan games. We're also pursuing games from other creators, helping their ideas become finished experiences.
